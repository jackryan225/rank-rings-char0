the results in this repository. We found none. A search cannot prove that there is none. All times are UTC, on

## What we searched for

- The claim: a countable regular rank ℚ-algebra with no unital ring homomorphism into any rank ultraproduct of matrix
  rings over fields of characteristic 0.
- The finite form: finitely many integer relations that hold in a regular rank algebra of characteristic 0, with no
  approximate solution by matrices over a field of characteristic 0, up to rank `εN`.
- The inequality for idempotents on a projective plane (line 1 of the mechanism in `README.md`), and its failure in a
  rank ring of characteristic 0.
- The other readings of the question: ℂ-algebras of countable dimension over ℂ, `*`-regular rings.

## Nearest known results

- Greenfeld, arXiv:2210.11650: stably finite algebras that are not linear sofic, over every field. By his Lemma 2.1
  every Sylvester rank function on them vanishes on a nonzero element (our deduction; not stated in the paper). So
  they have no faithful Sylvester rank function and do not embed into a regular rank ring. Stably finite algebras
  that are not linear sofic are therefore not new; what we found no earlier example of is one with a faithful
  Sylvester rank function.
- Bauer, Blachar, Greenfeld, arXiv:2401.04676, Example 2.4: the equations `xyz = 1`, `xzy = 0` have no approximate
  matrix solutions with normalized rank error below 1/4, over any field. These equations have no solution in a ring
  with a faithful rank function either, so they give no example of our kind.
- Yang, Hou, Fang, arXiv:2611.20690 (16 November 2026): an infinite dimensional regular `*`-algebra generated by
  three projections that embeds into every II₁ factor. It concerns the reading with countable dimension over ℂ and
  gives no counterexample.
- Rings of positive characteristic give trivial examples, for instance the field `𝔽_2` or rank completions of
  matrix rings over `𝔽_7`: they have no unital map into a ring that contains ℚ. This is why the claim is for
  ℚ-algebras.
- Mghirbi-Douglas, arXiv:2611.04599, and the OpenAI result on direct finiteness in characteristic 2: groups that are
  not linear sofic over fields of positive characteristic. Their group algebras are not directly finite, so they have
  no faithful rank function. The authors of 2611.04599 note that their method gives nothing over ℂ.
- MIP* = RE, arXiv:2001.04383: it should answer the question for `*`-regular rings and `*`-homomorphisms, by an
  argument of a few lines. We did not check that argument in detail. It is not in this repository.
- Dvir-Hu, arXiv:1412.0795, Theorem 1.4: the change of inner product in line 1 of the mechanism is a case of their
  result for real spaces. The inequality for idempotents, its failure in the `ℓ`-adic rank ring, and the
  consequences were not found in the literature.

## Remarks on the finite form

Two steps of the finite form are standard. If a ring has no unital map into any rank ultraproduct, a compactness
argument gives some finite set of integer relations without approximate matrix solutions. And for integer relations
a gap over ℂ gives the same gap over every field of characteristic 0. The new parts are the explicit relations
`B_ℓ`, their exact solutions over `𝔽_ℓ`, and the rank ring of characteristic 0 in which they hold.
